### Why this works

1. **`re.sub(r' {3,}', '-', text)`**  
   - The regular expression ` {3,}` matches any sequence of three or more spaces.  
   - All such sequences are replaced by a single hyphen (`-`).  
   - This handles runs of length 3, 4, 10, … in one pass.

2. **`.replace(' ', '_')`**  
   - After the first step, the only spaces that can remain are isolated (single) spaces.  
   - Converting them to underscores gives the exact output required by the docstring.

3. **Order matters** – we replace the long runs *before* converting the remaining spaces, otherwise the hyphens would be turned into underscores as well.
