## Model outputs

Aggregated evaluation results under `data/runs/aggregates/` summarize outputs
generated by third-party commercial and open-weight models (OpenAI, Anthropic,
Google, Zhipu, Moonshot, MiniMax, DeepSeek, Qwen) accessed through OpenRouter,
Cerebras, and W&B Inference. Short qualitative excerpts appear in
`data/runs/aggregates/examples/` for scientific commentary. Raw transcripts
are **not** distributed. Nothing in this repository grants rights over those
providers' model outputs; consult each provider's terms of service.
